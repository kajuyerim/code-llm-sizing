\newcommand{\DSDiscussionText}{%
\paragraph{A versus B} B was added after the three original conditions were analyzed. Its pooled 152/188 (80.9\%) was 3.2 points above none; original task-weighted B-none was +2.1 ($p=0.5774$). Pooled A-B was +4.8 points versus task-weighted +6.1 ($p=0.0129$). Both patches recorded the \code{undici} decision and passed both related checks in every run. A recorded more Maybe entries, including activity components and their data object; these observations do not identify which entry caused the gain.

\paragraph{Task variation} Routine tasks improved 82\% to 97\%, while knowledge tasks moved 74\% to 76\%, so benefits were not limited to cases where code is silent. On the Actual Budget webhook task, all five A runs imported and mocked \code{fetch} from \code{undici}; all ten none and seed runs used Node's global \code{fetch}. Other outcomes appear in Tables~\ref{tab:dstask} and~\ref{tab:dscat}. In Maybe, badge checks assumed a design-system component while the knowledge placed domain components under \code{UI::}. In Actual Budget, maintained-knowledge checks for keeping a new half-year interval out of three other reports fell from 60\% to 0\%; runs left reports untouched and said so in commit messages, but the judge could not confirm that from the diff. Those runs also took longer, and one committed a stray test configuration. These examples constrain interpretation, not a universal mechanism.

\paragraph{Seed, cost, and limitations} Seed knowledge described a Maybe component naming scheme that code had abandoned; maintenance corrected it. This does not establish that stale knowledge is always harmful or any knowledge file beneficial. Development mean cost rose from \$0.202 to \$0.240 per task. Separately, producing v2 A-Sonnet knowledge cost \$1.40 per application push; allocating it across ten later tasks would add \$0.14 each, an assumed reuse example rather than a measured amortized cost. Six tasks, five repetitions, one development model, simulated developers, and one diff-only judge limit generalization. The same team designed tasks and hidden checks; two checklist problems found after runs are reported without rewriting checks.}
