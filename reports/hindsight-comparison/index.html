What each study measured

The studies used different tasks, models, and scoring criteria. Each finding applies to the conditions of its own study.

Hindsight · SDE-Bench

Can a coding agent fix a bug while honoring a decision recorded earlier? SDE-Bench grades code patches on bug-fix tasks in boltons, including tests for prior decisions. Hindsight’s published results track coding-agent costs and correction rounds with and without memory.

Benchmark protocolPublished results

Context Ops · Feature sequence

Do decisions from earlier features carry into later sessions? The study followed chains of simulated feature tasks with no shared knowledge, repository knowledge, or team knowledge. It scored whether earlier decisions were followed and reported development and maintenance costs separately.

Study results and limitations

What would a shared evaluation require?

Use the same conversation history, repo revision, development task, agent, and scoring criteria. Measure both the knowledge produced and the resulting patch. Record ingestion or maintenance cost separately from retrieval and development cost, along with time and correction rounds. An amended rule and an application-specific exception are good cases for testing freshness and scope.
